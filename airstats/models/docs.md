Normal airports:
- 'small_airport':  A small airport
- 'medium_airport': A medium sized airport
- 'large_airport':  A large airport

Special airports:
- 'heliport':       A helicopter landing place
- 'seaplane_base'   A base for seaplanes
- 'balloonport'     A port for balloons

No longer active airports:
- 'closed'          Inactive airport
